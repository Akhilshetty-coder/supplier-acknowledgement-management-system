# AI Usage Disclosure



## 1. Purpose



AI tools were used as supporting resources during the development of the Supplier Acknowledgement Management System.



The project was developed through individual implementation, review, testing, and validation.



## 2. AI Tools Used



- ChatGPT

- Other AI coding assistant(s), where applicable



## 3. Areas Where AI Assisted



AI assistance was used for:



1\. Understanding the business requirements and project workflow.

2\. Breaking the project into manageable development operations.

3\. Understanding database relationships and foreign-key constraints.

4\. Explaining Python, SQLite, and business logic.

5\. Troubleshooting errors during development.

6\. Understanding synthetic-data generation and validation.

7\. Improving dashboard layout and usability.

8\. Preparing documentation and testing instructions.



## 4. Human Review and Responsibility



The project developer was responsible for:



- Creating and maintaining the project environment.

- Running Python scripts and database commands.

- Reviewing database schemas and relationships.

- Executing business logic tests.

- Generating and validating synthetic datasets.

- Checking database foreign-key integrity.

- Importing synthetic datasets into SQLite.

- Running and reviewing the Streamlit dashboard.

- Reviewing and adapting AI-assisted suggestions.



AI-generated suggestions were not treated as automatically correct. They were reviewed and tested against the actual project environment.



## 5. Testing Evidence



The following verification results were obtained during development:



- Synthetic dataset validation: 39 checks passed, 0 failed.

- Synthetic data import completed successfully.

- Database foreign-key errors: 0.

- Four anomaly scenarios were included in the synthetic ground-truth dataset.

- Three invalid source records were quarantined in DataQualityException.



## 6. Limitations



AI assistance does not replace developer understanding or testing.



The developer is responsible for explaining the implementation, business rules, design decisions, limitations, and test results during evaluation.



## 7. Declaration



AI tools were used as development-support tools for learning, planning, troubleshooting, reviewing, and documentation. The developer reviewed and validated the project implementation and takes responsibility for the submitted work.
